# Approximate UK telephone / postcode associations

`uk_locations.json` is a bundled, offline reference generated by `scripts/build_location_reference.py`. It includes 771 geographic telephone codes/prefixes and 3,002 postcode districts; 684 telephone entries have a postcode-area association in this snapshot. The remainder intentionally have no inferred coordinate or postcode association.

## Sources and attribution

- [Ofcom telephone area-code tool](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/phone-numbers/telephone-area-codes-tool): factual code/place pairs, retrieved 26 September 2026. Includes extended prefixes for shared dialling codes. [Ofcom copyright and reuse](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/about-ofcom/our-website/copyright-and-information-re-use).
- [GeoNames GB postal data](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/GB.zip), retrieved 26 September 2026. [Format / licence](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/readme.txt), [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Data has been transformed into district representative points and joined to Ofcom place names. Data is provided without accuracy/completeness guarantees.

## Interpretation

These are **town-based associations, not a telephone exchange footprint or an authoritative postcode crosswalk**. Exact place-name matches produce candidate postcode areas and sample districts. Normalized spellings, explicit place aliases and postcode-area/region disambiguation rules are recorded in the generator. Towns with geographically separated namesakes are otherwise left unmapped. A representative point is the mean of the matching postal place records, not the incident address.

London explicitly associates with its eight London postal areas; this omits some outer 020 coverage. Shared codes such as 0191, 023 and 028 aggregate candidate associations and have no point coordinate. Telephone number portability and VoIP mean even a valid geographic code cannot establish the physical location of a caller or a broadband line. Crown Dependency and unmapped localities remain visible by name but may lack postal data.

Only geographically contextualized standalone codes are extracted from incident text. Support/contact clauses and full telephone numbers are ignored. `+44` and `0044` code notation is normalized. Full postcodes can identify a district representative point; bare outcodes require postal context or a source region field. Source coordinates always take priority. Several codes can produce several approximate points for a single incident; this does not increase the incident count. Conflicting postcode and telephone associations are flagged.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/build_location_reference.py`; inspect the resulting coverage and diff before adopting updated upstream data. No runtime geocoding requests or customer phone-number lookups are performed.
